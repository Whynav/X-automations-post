# AGENCY POSITIONING & OPERATIONAL ENGINE

## 1. Identity & Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- **Target:** B2B AI SaaS, Developer Tools, and Tech Startups.
- **The Funding Trigger:** Startups that raised a round 2.5 to 3.5 months ago. They have runway, need immediate growth, and feel executive/board pressure to show organic traction and brand footprint.
- **The Core Offer:** Organic Reddit Distribution & LLM Citation Engineering (AEO/GEO).
- **The Performance Guarantee (The Hook):** "1 Million impressions in 2 months or we work for free until we hit it."
- **Custom Pricing:** Custom pricing per client based on volume and scope ($2,500–$5,000+/mo retainer; $500–$1,000 paid entry audit).

## 2. Core Pain Points (Nerves That Keep Funded Founders Awake)
- **The Brand Void:** When prospective buyers search `"{startup name} review reddit"`, nothing shows up. Or worse, Google's AI Search Overview hallucinates irrelevant garbage miles away from what the software actually does.
- **The LLM Hijack:** A buyer asks ChatGPT or Perplexity a category query (e.g. "best AI agent builder"). The LLM cites a Reddit thread where the client's competitor was recommended. The competitor captures authority and pipeline simply because they existed in a Reddit thread the LLM trusted.
- **The Defensive Reputation Crisis:** One angry developer complains about the client's API on `r/webdev`. That single thread ranks number one on Google for their brand name. They lack the organic karma infrastructure to counter the narrative.
- **The CAC Apocalypse:** The startup just raised a seed round. Meta and LinkedIn ads burn through their runway with sky-high acquisition costs. They desperately need organic baseline traffic to survive the next board meeting.
- **The Ghost Town Competitor Hijack:** A direct competitor ships an inferior product, but that competitor dominates organic Reddit discussions. ChatGPT defaults to recommending the competitor purely based on comment density.

## 3. The Enemies (Specific Targets to Throw Rocks At)
- **Bot-Driven Upvote Farms:** Shill agencies using fake upvotes that get client domains permanently blacklisted by Reddit moderators.
- **The Link-Spam Automation Bros:** Script kiddies using Make or n8n to auto-reply to Reddit threads with competitor links. Moderators shadowban these domains within 48 hours.
- **Founder Ego-Posters:** Founders writing long diary entries in `r/SaaS` expecting conversions. They get vanity upvotes, but zero pipeline.
- **Brand-Safety Cowards:** Legal or PR teams watering down Reddit copy until it sounds like a corporate press release. Redditors smell corporate fear instantly and downvote it to oblivion.
- **Product Hunt Launchers:** Founders blowing $10k on upvote groups for a 24-hour dopamine spike. The next day their traffic flatlines.
- **Lazy SEO Agencies:** Selling 6-month timeline blog posts that zero humans read and LLMs ignore.
- **Legacy PR Firms:** Charging $10k for a Forbes article that drives zero software trials or API signups.

## 4. Our Secret Sauce (In-the-Trenches Delivery Mechanics)
- **High-CQS Infrastructure & Account-Subreddit Affinity:** Exclusively using aged accounts with high Contributor Quality Score (CQS). A veteran account means nothing if it only posted in gaming subreddits; we map accounts specifically to developer and SaaS communities months before deploying them for B2B campaigns.
- **The Inception Framework:** We never just drop a link. We deploy an aged account to ask a highly specific technical question. Four hours later another account provides a massive value-driven answer. The client's brand naturally fits inside the solution block.
- **Downvote Shielding:** The first 15 minutes dictate a thread's survival. We monitor early sentiment velocity. If the community turns hostile, we pivot the comment before negative domain association triggers automod filters.
- **Semantic LLM Bait:** Perplexity and ChatGPT look for distinct syntactical patterns to extract answers. We write Reddit comments matching those exact extraction patterns to force the Answer Engine to cite our clients.
- **100% Rule Compliance:** Adhering strictly to subreddit rules. Prioritizing subtle, unlinked, natural brand mentions over spammy anchor links.
- **4-Tier Tracking:**
  1. Thread survival rate (>95% unremoved after 14 days).
  2. Google SERP ranking for `"{keyword} reddit"`.
  3. Direct citation presence in Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Gemini.
  4. Branded search and referral lift.

## 5. Proof Vectors (Building Authority at Early Stage)
- **YC-Backed Campaign Insights:** Leveraging operational data and lessons learned from past campaigns running distribution for 2–3 YC-backed startups (focusing on campaign data and mechanics, not client logos).
- **Public Subreddit Teardowns:** Scraping and dissecting why specific SaaS launches exploded with 1k+ upvotes vs. why others were banned in 15 minutes.
- **Live AEO Experiments:** Documenting live tests showing how a Reddit comment directly alters a Perplexity or Gemini output 48 hours later.
- **Automod Autopsies:** Pulling public automod regex patterns and showing how legitimate brands trigger spam filters.
