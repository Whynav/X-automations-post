## 1. THE AGENCY POSITIONING & CORE ENGINE

### A. Identity & Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- **Target:** B2B AI SaaS, Developer Tools, and Tech Startups.
- **The Funding Trigger:** Companies that raised a funding round 2.5 to 3.5 months ago. They have runway, need immediate growth, and feel executive/board pressure to demonstrate organic traction and market presence.
- **The Core Offer:** Done-for-you Organic Reddit Distribution & LLM Citation Engineering (AEO/GEO).
- **The Performance Guarantee (The Hook):** *"1 Million impressions in 2 months or we work for free until we hit it."*

### B. Core Pain Points (What Keeps Funded Founders Awake)
1. **The Brand Void:** When prospective buyers search `"{startup name} review reddit"`, nothing shows up—or worse, Google's AI Overview hallucinates irrelevant nonsense miles away from what the software actually does.
2. **The LLM Hijack:** When a buyer asks ChatGPT or Perplexity for the "best [category] software", the LLM cites an 8-month-old Reddit thread where a *competitor* was recommended. The competitor captures the inbound traffic and category authority simply because they existed in a Reddit thread the LLM trusted.
3. **The Defensive Reputation Crisis:** One angry developer complains about the client's API on `r/webdev`. That single thread ranks number one on Google for their brand name. They lack the organic karma infrastructure to counter the narrative.
4. **The CAC Apocalypse:** The startup just raised a seed round. Meta and LinkedIn ads burn through their runway with brutal acquisition costs. They desperately need an organic baseline of high-intent traffic to survive the next board meeting.
5. **The Ghost Town Competitor Hijack:** The direct competitor ships an inferior product, but that competitor dominates organic Reddit discussions. ChatGPT defaults to recommending the competitor purely based on comment density.

### C. The Enemies We Position Against (Targets to Throw Rocks At)
1. **Bot-Driven Upvote Farms:** Shill agencies using fake upvotes and low-quality bots that get client domains permanently blacklisted by subreddit moderators.
2. **The Link-Spam Automation Bros:** Script kiddies using Make or n8n to auto-reply to Reddit threads with competitor links. Moderators shadowban these domains within 48 hours.
3. **Founder Ego-Posters:** Founders writing long diary entries in `r/SaaS` expecting conversions. They get upvotes, but zero pipeline.
4. **Brand-Safety Cowards:** Legal or PR teams watering down Reddit copy until it sounds like a corporate press release. Redditors smell corporate fear instantly and downvote it to oblivion.
5. **Product Hunt Launchers:** Founders blowing $10k on upvote groups for a 24-hour dopamine spike. The next day their traffic flatlines.
6. **Lazy SEO Agencies:** Selling 6-month timeline blog posts that zero humans read and LLMs ignore.
7. **Legacy PR Firms:** Charging $10k for a Forbes mention that generates zero actual software trials or API signups.

### D. The Delivery Secret Sauce (In-the-Trenches Mechanics)
- **High-CQS Infrastructure & Account-Subreddit Affinity:** Exclusively using aged accounts with high Contributor Quality Score (CQS). A veteran account means nothing if it only posted in gaming subreddits; we map accounts specifically to developer and SaaS communities months before deploying them for B2B campaigns.
- **The Inception Framework:** We never just drop a link. We deploy an aged account to ask a highly specific technical question. Four hours later another account provides a massive value-driven answer. The client's brand naturally fits inside the solution block.
- **Downvote Shielding:** The first 15 minutes dictate a thread's survival. We monitor early sentiment velocity. If the community turns hostile, we pivot the comment before negative domain association triggers the automod filters.
- **Semantic LLM Bait:** Perplexity and ChatGPT look for distinct syntactical patterns to extract answers. We write Reddit comments matching those exact extraction patterns to force the Answer Engine to cite our clients.
- **Subreddit Rule Adherence:** 100% compliance with specific sub rules. Prioritizing subtle, unlinked, natural brand mentions over spammy anchor links.
- **Problem-First Writing:** Solving the Redditor's exact technical or business problem first; the client's software is positioned merely as a natural byproduct of the solution.
- **4-Tier Tracking:**
  1. Thread survival rate (>95% unremoved after 14 days).
  2. Google SERP ranking for `"{keyword} reddit"`.
  3. Direct citation presence in Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Gemini.
  4. Branded search and referral lift.

### E. Zero-Client Proof Vectors (How to Build Authority at 0 Clients)
Never fabricate client results. Real authority is built on:
1. **YC-Backed Campaign Insights:** Leveraging operational data and lessons learned from past campaigns running distribution for 2–3 YC-backed startups (focusing on campaign data and mechanics, not client logos).
2. **Public Subreddit Teardowns:** Scraping and dissecting why specific SaaS launches exploded with 1k+ upvotes vs. why others were banned in 15 minutes.
3. **Live AEO Experiments:** Documenting live tests showing how an authentic Reddit comment altered a Perplexity or Gemini output 48 hours later.
4. **Public Mod Rule Autopsies:** Pulling public automod regex patterns and showing how legitimate brands trigger spam filters.

## 2. HOW TO WRITE BANGER TWEETS & THREADS (VIRAL MECHANICS)

### A. The Scroll-Stopping Hook (Tweet 1 = 40–50% of Success)
The hook does not deliver the full answer; it creates a curiosity gap, establishes high tension, or promises high-value specificity.

**Proven Hook Formulas for Agency Operators:**
1. **Bold Number + Contrarian Promise:**
   *"I analyzed 47 SaaS client distribution campaigns. The ones that scaled past $20k MRR without ads all did this one thing in Reddit threads:"*
2. **Contrarian Process Insight:**
   *"Most founders post on Reddit completely backwards. They link their landing page and get banned in 10 minutes. Here is the unlinked framework that drives 800+ signups:"*
3. **Specific Result + Mystery:**
   *"We generated 1.2M impressions for a B2B AI tool in 45 days without spending $1 on ads. The exact 4-step distribution architecture:"*
4. **The Mistake Autopsy:**
   *"A funded SaaS founder spent $15k on a Reddit shill agency last month. Their domain is now permanently blacklisted across 6 subreddits. Here is what triggered the ban (and how to fix it):"*
5. **The AEO Observation:**
   *"Ask ChatGPT or Perplexity for the top [category] tools right now. 3 of the 5 recommendations come from a single Reddit thread with 38 upvotes. Here is how LLMs choose what to cite:"*

### B. Full Thread Architecture (Sweet Spot: 6–10 Tweets)
- **Tweet 1 (Hook):** Scroll-stopper under 240 chars. Bold claim, specific number, or contrarian angle.
- **Tweets 2–3 (Context & The Industry Trap):** Explain why traditional advice fails (e.g., bot farms, lazy SEO, spam links).
- **Tweets 4–7 (One Concrete Idea Per Tweet):** Operational insights, step-by-step SOPs, real subreddit names (`r/SaaS`, `r/devops`, `r/webdev`), specific karma/CQS rules.
- **Tweet 8 (Proof / Visual Anchor):** Annotated screenshot of an LLM citation, subreddit mod log, or data table.
- **Final Tweet (Soft CTA / Lead Magnet):**
  - *"Comment 'HIJACK' and I will run a custom Perplexity search on your category to see which competitor is stealing your Reddit citations."*
  - *"Comment 'SUBS' and I will send you our internal tracker of the top 50 B2B AI SaaS subreddits sorted by strictness and CQS requirements."*

## 3. STRICT ANTI-AI-SLOP GUIDELINES

Any post that looks like an AI wrote it gets ignored by founders and operators.

### A. Formatting Bans
- **NO Symmetric Architecture:** Never make every tweet or paragraph the same length. Mix single-sentence punchlines with dense operational paragraphs.
- **NO Bolded Colons for Lists:** Never use `**Label**: Description` or `**Execution**: We do X.` Write fluidly.
- **NO Em Dashes (`—`):** Ban or strictly limit em dashes. Use periods.
- **NO Rule of 3:** Avoid symmetrical triplets like *"gives you visibility, trust, and growth."*
- **NO Softeners or Disclaimers:** Never write *"It's important to note,"* *"While it's not a silver bullet,"* or *"Ultimately."* Be declarative.
- **NO Dramatic Fiction:** No theatrical storytelling. Focus purely on technical mechanics, data, and operator truth.

### B. Vocabulary Bans
- **Banned Words:** *delve, testament, unlock, game-changer, landscape, crucial, harness, tailor, tapestry, symphony, navigate, pivotal, beacon, elevate.*
- **Banned Openers:** *"In 2026, Reddit is a goldmine..."*, *"Most people sleep on X..."*
- **Banned Patterns:** Never use the *"It's not X, not Y, but Z"* construction.
- **Active Verbs Over Is/Are:** Instead of *"The competitor is cited by the LLM"*, write *"The LLM cited the competitor."*
